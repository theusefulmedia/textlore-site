    02 Keep

    Messages lets old photos go. Textlore keeps its own copy.

    When your Mac runs low on space, Messages in iCloud removes the copies of photos and files on your Mac. Textlore saves every message and photo into its own folder the moment it arrives, so a photo from today is still here in 10 years.

    The archive starts the day you install Textlore. Photos already removed show their preview and an Open in Messages button. One-time codes are skipped if you've told Messages to delete them.

    Questions

    Does it include texts from my iPhone?
    Yes, with Messages in iCloud switched on on your iPhone and your Mac. Textlore reads the Messages history on your Mac, which then includes your iPhone and iPad conversations. You never need to plug your iPhone in.
    Does it work with WhatsApp?
    Not yet. Textlore reads Apple's Messages app: iMessage and SMS.
    Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?
    macOS keeps your Messages history in a protected folder and only opens it to apps you switch on. Textlore only reads it.
    Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?
    Apple doesn't let Mac App Store apps read the Messages history, so every app like this is sold directly.
    Can it show photos that are only in iCloud?
    It shows the preview Messages keeps on your Mac, and a button that opens the photo in Messages. From now on, the archive saves each photo while it's on your Mac.
    What happens after 14 days?
    Textlore asks for a licence. Your archive keeps saving in the background while you decide, so nothing is lost.
    Can I use it on my MacBook and my iMac?
    Yes. One licence works on 2 Macs.
