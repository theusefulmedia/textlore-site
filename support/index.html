1Moving a licence to a new Mac

If both places on your licence are taken, email your licence key or your Gumroad receipt and say which Mac you've stopped using. Raja frees a place for you.

2What to include

Your Mac model, your macOS version, and the Textlore version (Textlore > About Textlore). Please don't send screenshots of your messages; Textlore never needs them to fix a problem.
