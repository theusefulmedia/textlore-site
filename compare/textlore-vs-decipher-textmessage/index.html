Textlore vs Decipher TextMessage, side by side.

Both are one-time purchases, and both save your texts as PDF and CSV. Decipher is built around saving and printing messages from iPhone backups. Textlore is built around your iMessage and WhatsApp history on your Mac: what friends told you to try, what you promised, and search, an archive and Your Lore.

Short answer

Pick Decipher TextMessage if your texts are on an iPhone you back up to a computer, you use Windows, or your main goal is a printout of a conversation. Pick Textlore if your messages are on your Mac through Messages in iCloud or WhatsApp for Mac and you want to see what friends told you to try and what you promised, search them every day, keep an archive that saves as you go, and export them with private details hidden.

Where Decipher TextMessage is the better choice

  • It reads iPhone backups. Back up your iPhone to a Mac or PC and Decipher reads the messages from it, so Messages in iCloud doesn't have to be on.
  • It runs on Windows. Decipher works on Windows 7 and later as well as the Mac. Textlore is Mac only.
  • It runs on older Macs. Decipher supports macOS Sierra and later. Textlore needs macOS 26.
  • Deleted messages. Its free trial can show whether deleted messages are still in a backup and can be recovered.
  • A similar price. $29.99 once, against Textlore's $20 launch price until 28 October. After that Textlore is $39.A lower price. $29.99 once, against Textlore's $39 once.

Where Textlore is the better choice

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your Messages and WhatsApp for Mac history. Recs lists what friends told you to try, with their exact words, and Promises lists what you said you'd do for people, both found by Apple's on-device model on Macs with Apple Intelligence. It also searches every year as you type, keeps its own archive of messages and photos, exports to 6 formats with names and numbers hidden, and adds Your Lore, your texting years as tree rings, and Ask AI. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Recs and Promises. Every book, show, restaurant and app friends told you to try, with who said it and their words, and every promise you made, with how many days late it is. Decipher doesn't list anything like them.
  • WhatsApp next to your iMessages. Textlore reads WhatsApp for Mac, and search, Ask AI and export cover it too. Decipher offers WhatsApp in a separate app, Decipher Chat.
  • Search you will use every day. Results narrow as you type, with filters such as from:, in:, during: and has:photo, and ⌥Space searches from any app.
  • Up to date by itself. Textlore reads your Mac's Messages history and updates within seconds when a new text arrives, with no backup to refresh.
  • An archive that keeps going. It saves every message and photo while it's on your Mac, so they stay after Messages deletes them or clears space.
  • Private exports with a preview. Phone numbers and emails are hidden unless you switch them on, names can be initials or hidden, and a live preview shows the file before you save.
  • Photos and files by person and month. The Attachments page shows everything people sent you in one place.
  • Your Lore and Ask AI. Your texting years as tree rings with share cards that never show message text, and answers from Apple's on-device model on Macs with Apple Intelligence.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • Recs, Promises and Ask AI need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Which one should you pick?

  • Your texts are only in an iPhone backup, or you use Windows or an older Mac: Decipher TextMessage.
  • You mainly need a printout of one conversation from your iPhone: Decipher TextMessage.
  • Your texts are on your Mac and you want to search, keep and export them all the time: Textlore.
  • You want to see what friends told you to try and what you promised: Textlore.
  • You want photos by person, private exports and a look back at your years of texting: Textlore.

For printing, both make a PDF. Our guide on printing text messages for court covers what to include, whichever app you use.

How much does Decipher TextMessage cost?

Its product page lists $29.99 as a one-time purchase with no subscription, including 2 device activations. Textlore is $20 until 28 October, then $39$39 once, for 2 Macs.

Does Decipher TextMessage work on a Mac without an iPhone backup?

Its product page says it also saves and exports text messages from the Messages app on a Mac. Textlore only reads the Mac's Messages history.

Can Textlore read an iPhone backup?

No. Switch on Messages in iCloud on your iPhone and your Mac so your iPhone conversations are on the Mac, and Textlore reads them there.

Can either app recover deleted texts?

Decipher's trial can show whether deleted messages are still in an iPhone backup. Textlore can't bring back messages that were gone before you installed it, but it keeps its own copy of everything after that.
