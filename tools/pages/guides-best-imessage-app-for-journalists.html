{{icon:search}}Find what a source said, and when

Press ⌘F and type. Results narrow with every letter and your words light up in amber. Add from:Meera for one source, in:Newsroom for one group, before:, after: or during: for dates, and quotes for an exact phrase. Opening a result shows the conversation around it, with the date headings.

{{shot:search|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|Textlore search for flight lands, with matches across conversations and the words lit up in amber.}}

Textlore keeps its own copy of messages and photos while they are on your Mac, so a text stays searchable even after Messages clears space. Ask AI, on Macs with Apple Intelligence, answers questions such as "When did Meera send the document?" with Apple's on-device model, and lists the messages it used. Check the quote in the message itself before you publish.

{{icon:export}}Export an excerpt without exposing a number

Select a few messages with ⇧-click, press ⌘E, and choose PDF, text, Markdown, CSV or an image. Choose names in full, as initials or hidden. Phone numbers and emails are hidden by default. A live preview shows the file before you save it.

{{shot:export-options|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|The Textlore export sheet with a live preview of the PDF.}}

Use that for a fact-check file, an editor or a legal review. Keep the originals, and the phone, as they are.

{{icon:promise}}Promises: what you told sources you would do

Promises lists every time you said you would do something for someone, with how many days late it is and the message behind it. It is a short list of the things sources are waiting on. It never sends a message. It works on a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

{{icon:lock}}What Textlore does not do

  • It does not encrypt anything or make a conversation safer. Source protection depends on how you message and how you secure your Mac.
  • It does not recover messages that were already gone before you installed it.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • Recs, Promises and Ask AI need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.
