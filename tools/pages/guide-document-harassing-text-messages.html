{{icon:shield}}1. Stay safe first

If you feel unsafe, call your local emergency number. Police can tell you what they need from the texts.

You can block the sender in Messages and still keep what they already sent. Blocking stops new messages. It does not delete the old ones.

{{icon:keep}}2. Keep the texts, out of sight

  • Set Keep messages to Forever on your Mac and iPhone.
  • Don't reply, and don't delete. Mute the conversation if you don't want to see it.
  • Install Textlore and leave its archive on. It keeps a copy of every message on your Mac, so the record survives even if the conversation is deleted later.

{{icon:export}}3. Export a dated record

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your whole Messages history at once. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  1. Open the conversation in Textlore. You don't need to read it: choose the date range or press ⇧⌘E for all of it.
  2. Choose PDF, timestamps on, phone numbers and emails on.
  3. Save the PDF in 2 safe places. Add new messages by exporting again later.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Should I delete harassing texts?

Most guidance says keep them, because they are your record. You can block and mute the sender so you don't see new ones.

Does blocking delete old messages?

No. Blocking stops new messages. The old conversation stays until you delete it.

Can I export messages from several numbers?

Yes. Export each conversation, or search across all chats with a date range and export the results you select.

Can the sender see that I exported?

No. Textlore only reads your Mac's copy and sends nothing.

Who should I give the record to?

The police, a lawyer, your employer or school, depending on the case. They will tell you what format they need.
