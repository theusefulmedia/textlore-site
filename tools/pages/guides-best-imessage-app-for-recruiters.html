{{icon:promise}}Promises: every follow-up you owe a candidate

Candidates remember who called back and who did not. Promises lists every time you said you would do something for someone, with the day you said it and how many days late it is. Each has I kept this, Remind me and Open in Messages. Apple's on-device model finds them on a Mac with Apple Intelligence. Textlore never sends a message, so you keep every promise yourself.

{{shot:promises|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|Promises in Textlore: 13 of 15 still open, with Call Grandma on her birthday 55 days late and the message where you said it.}}
  • Open the Promises page on Monday and clear the ones past their day first.
  • Click a promise to read the message where you made it, with the conversation around it.
  • Tick I kept this when it is done. Textlore also flags ones where a later message suggests you kept it.

{{icon:search}}Search: what a candidate told you, months later

Searching is built into Textlore for every year at once. Press ⌘F and type. Narrow it with search words: from:Priya for what she sent you, in:Hiring group for one group, after:january 2026 for a date, "notice period" for an exact phrase. Press ⌥Space from any app to search without opening Textlore.

{{shot:search|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|Textlore search for flight lands, with matches across conversations and the words lit up in amber.}}

{{icon:export}}Export a candidate thread

  1. Open the conversation and press ⇧⌘E.
  2. Choose PDF or CSV. Pick timestamps, names in full or as initials, and whether phone numbers show. They are hidden by default.
  3. Check the live preview, then export.

Check your employer's policy before you store candidate messages outside your CRM, and before you move work conversations onto a personal Mac.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • Recs, Promises and Ask AI need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.
  • It does not send, schedule or bulk text anyone.
  • It does not connect to an applicant tracking system or a CRM.
  • One licence covers 1 person on 2 Macs, not a team.
