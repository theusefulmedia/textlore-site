How to save text messages from someone who died, so they are never lost.

Their texts can feel like the last place you hear their voice. Phones get replaced and settings delete old messages, so it's worth saving them properly, once, when you're ready.

Updated 24 November 2026. For Messages on macOS 26 and later.

{{icon:shield}}1. The 1 thing to do now

Make sure nothing deletes on its own. That is all that needs doing today.

  1. On your iPhone: Settings > Apps > Messages > Keep Messages > Forever.
  2. On your Mac: Messages > Settings > General > Keep messages > Forever.

Don't delete the conversation, even by accident while tidying. Muting it is fine.

{{icon:keep}}2. Save the whole conversation

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your whole Messages history at once. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  1. Install Textlore and leave its archive on. It keeps its own copy of every message and every photo or voice message that is on your Mac.
  2. Open their conversation and press ⇧⌘E. Choose PDF with the bubble style, so it looks like your phone, and photos full size.
  3. Save the PDF in 2 places, for example your Mac and iCloud Drive or a USB drive.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Will their messages disappear if their phone number is cancelled?

Messages already on your devices stay. What deletes them is your Keep Messages setting, or deleting the conversation. Set it to Forever.

Can I get messages from their phone or iCloud?

That depends on Apple's rules. Apple's Digital Legacy lets a chosen Legacy Contact request access. Textlore works with the messages on your own Mac.

Can I save their voice messages?

Yes, the ones on your Mac. Textlore keeps a copy in its archive, and you can find them in Attachments. See our guide to saving photos and voice messages.

Can I do this later?

Yes. Once Keep Messages is set to Forever, nothing deletes on its own. Save the conversation when you feel ready.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Textlore never sends your messages anywhere.
