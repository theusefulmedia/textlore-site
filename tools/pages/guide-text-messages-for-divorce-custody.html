{{icon:shield}}1. Keep everything, from today

  • Set Keep messages to Forever in Messages on your Mac and iPhone.
  • Don't delete or edit anything, even messages that make you look bad. Your lawyer needs the whole picture.
  • Keep Textlore's archive on, so photos and messages stay saved on your Mac even if Messages offloads them.

{{icon:search}}2. Find the messages that matter

Textlore searches years of texts as you type. Filters help you get to the point:

  • from: a person, and a date like after:March 2025.
  • Words like pickup, school, payment, weekend or doctor.
  • Promises lists commitments made in your chats, like "I'll pay you back Friday", with the date.

{{icon:export}}3. Export a dated timeline

  1. Open the conversation and choose the period your lawyer asked about.
  2. Export as PDF with timestamps, date headings and phone numbers on.
  3. Export as CSV too, if your lawyer wants to sort by date.

Textlore exports WhatsApp chats you import as well, if your co-parent also messages you there.

{{icon:help}}Questions

How far back can Textlore go?

As far as your Messages history on the Mac goes. With Messages in iCloud, that is usually years.

Can I export only messages about the children?

Search for the words that matter, then select those messages with their context and export them. Your lawyer may still want the full conversation.

Will the other person know I exported the texts?

No. Textlore never sends anything and makes no network calls with your messages.

Can I include photos they sent?

Yes, when the photos are on your Mac. Textlore can include them full size or small.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is practical help. Your lawyer decides what to use and how.
