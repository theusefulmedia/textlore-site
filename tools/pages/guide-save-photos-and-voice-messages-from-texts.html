{{icon:bubble}}1. Save them in Messages

  1. Open the conversation, click the name at the top, and open Photos.
  2. Select the photos you want and drag them into a folder in Finder.
  3. For a voice message, Control-click it and choose Save to Downloads if your Mac offers it, or keep it in the conversation.

Old files with a cloud badge are stored in iCloud. Open them once so the Mac downloads them.

{{icon:photo}}2. Find every file at once with Textlore

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your whole Messages history at once. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Attachments shows every photo, video, voice note and file in a grid, by person and date.
  • The archive copies each file into Textlore's own folder the moment it's on your Mac, so it stays even if Messages later offloads it.
  • Files that are only in iCloud show as a placeholder with an Open in Messages button, so you can bring them back.

{{icon:keep}}3. Back them up

  • Copy the folder to an external drive or iCloud Drive.
  • Turn on Time Machine if you haven't, so your Mac and Textlore's archive are both backed up.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Why do old photos in Messages look blurry or missing?

With Messages in iCloud, your Mac removes local copies when space runs low and keeps a small preview. Opening the photo downloads it again.

Does Textlore download photos from iCloud?

No. It keeps every file that is on your Mac. Use Open in Messages to bring an iCloud-only file back, and Textlore keeps it from then on.

Can I save voice messages?

Yes, the ones on your Mac. Textlore lists them with the other attachments and keeps a copy.

Does Textlore keep files after I delete the conversation?

Its archive keeps what it has already saved, unless you switch the archive off or delete Textlore's data.

Does any of this leave my Mac?

No. Everything stays on your Mac.
