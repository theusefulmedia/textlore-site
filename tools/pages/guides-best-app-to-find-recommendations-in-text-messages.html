{{icon:bubble}}1. Search the Messages app

  1. Open Messages and click the search field at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Type a phrase friends use, such as you should watch, you have to try or recommend, and press Return.
  3. Click each result to read it in its conversation, and copy the titles into a note.

It works, and it is free, but every phrase is a separate search and you build the list by hand. Messages has no date filter.

{{icon:search}}2. Search with filters in Textlore

Press ⌘F and combine words. from:Theo "you should" finds what Theo told you. "you have to" during:2025 narrows to a year. from:Priya has:link finds links she sent. Results narrow as you type across every year.
