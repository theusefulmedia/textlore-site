How to keep your text messages forever, on your Mac.

Messages can delete texts after 30 days or a year, and Messages in iCloud removes local copies of photos when space runs low. Here are the settings that stop it, and a copy that's yours.

{{icon:shield}}1. Change 2 settings

  1. Mac: Messages > Settings > General > Keep messages > Forever.
  2. iPhone: Settings > Apps > Messages > Keep Messages > Forever.

Check the same setting on any iPad too. With Messages in iCloud, deleting on 1 device deletes everywhere.

{{icon:cloud}}2. Know the iCloud catch

With Messages in iCloud on, your Mac may remove local copies of old photos and files to save space. They stay in iCloud, but only while your iCloud account and storage plan stay the same.

{{icon:keep}}3. Keep your own archive

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your whole Messages history at once. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  • The archive keeps every message and every attachment file it finds, even after Messages deletes or offloads them.
  • It respects "Delete verification codes after use", so 1-time codes are not kept.
  • Search years of texts in milliseconds, and export any conversation to PDF, HTML, text, Markdown or CSV.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Does Messages delete old texts by default?

Keep Messages can be set to 30 days, 1 year or Forever. Check it, because some setups use a shorter time.

Is Messages in iCloud a backup?

Not quite. It syncs. Delete a conversation on 1 device and it goes on all of them.

How much space does Textlore's archive use?

It depends on your photos. Textlore shows the archive's size and warns you when your Mac is low on space.

Can I switch the archive off?

Yes, in Settings > Archive.

What does Textlore Forever mean?
