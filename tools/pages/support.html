{{icon:key}}Moving a licence to a new Mac

On the old Mac, choose Settings > Licence > Deactivate This Mac. If both places on your licence are taken and the old Mac is gone, email your licence key or your Gumroad receipt and say which Mac you've stopped using. Raja frees a place for you.

{{icon:doc}}What to include

Your Mac model, your macOS version, and the Textlore version (Textlore > About Textlore).

{{icon:lock}}Please don't send screenshots of your messages. Textlore never needs them to fix a problem.
