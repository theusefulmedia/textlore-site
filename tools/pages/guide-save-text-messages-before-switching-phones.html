A new iPhone usually brings your messages over. A move to Android doesn't. Either way, a copy on your Mac means years of texts don't depend on 1 transfer going right.

{{icon:doc}}Quick answer

Before you switch, make sure Messages in iCloud has synced to your Mac. Keep a copy of the history with Textlore or export the conversations that matter. If you move to Android, turn off iMessage first so texts reach your new phone.

{{icon:laptop}}1. Get everything onto your Mac

  1. On the iPhone: Settings > your name > iCloud > Show All, and turn on Messages in iCloud.
  2. On the Mac: Messages > Settings > iMessage, tick Enable Messages in iCloud, and click Sync Now.
  3. Wait until old conversations show on the Mac. A big history can take hours.

{{icon:keep}}2. Keep a copy that's yours

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your whole Messages history at once. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Install Textlore and let it import. Its archive keeps every message and file on your Mac, whatever happens on the new phone.
  • Export the conversations that matter most as PDF, so you can read them anywhere.

{{icon:out}}3. Moving to Android? Turn off iMessage

  • Before you move your SIM, open Settings > Apps > Messages and turn off iMessage.
  • If you already switched, use Apple's Deregister iMessage page with your phone number.
  • Otherwise texts from iPhone friends can keep going to iMessage and never reach you.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Will my texts move to Android?

Some transfer tools move texts, but often without photos, reactions or group chats. A copy on your Mac is a safe extra.

Do I need to keep my old iPhone?

Keep it until you've checked that everything is on your Mac and the new phone.

Can I still search old iMessages after moving to Android?

Yes, on your Mac with Textlore, as long as the history is on the Mac.

What about WhatsApp?

Export your WhatsApp chats and import them into Textlore to search them in the same place.

Is a Mac needed?

Yes. Textlore is a Mac app that reads the Messages history on your Mac.
