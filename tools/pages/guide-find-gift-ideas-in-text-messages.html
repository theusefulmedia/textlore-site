{{icon:bubble}}1. Search the Messages app

  1. Open Messages and click the search field at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Type a phrase people use when they want something: I want, wish I had, need a new, obsessed with or my birthday.
  3. Click a result to read it in its conversation, and note the gift in a list.

It is free. The catch is noise: “I want pizza” and “I want that lamp” look the same to Messages, and it can't limit results to 1 person or 1 year.

{{icon:search}}2. Search one person in Textlore

Press ⌘F and add search words. Each one narrows the list as you type:

  • "I want" from:Mom during:2026 finds what Mom said she wanted this year.
  • "wish I had" from:Jordan finds every wish Jordan sent you.
  • has:link from:Priya after:september finds the product links Priya sent since September.
  • "need a new" -phone skips the lines about phones.

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your Messages and WhatsApp for Mac history. Recs lists what friends told you to try, including things to buy, with who said it, when and their exact words. Apple's on-device model finds them, so nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Things to buy. Products sit in Recs next to books and shows. Use Search recs to find one, then open the message it came from.
  • Who said it. A gift idea from your sister and a product tip from a coworker sit apart, each with a name.
  • Mark as tried. Tick off what you bought, so next year's list starts clean.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • It only reads. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.
