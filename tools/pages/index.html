What is Textlore?

Textlore is a Mac app that reads the iMessage and WhatsApp for Mac history on your Mac. It lists the things your friends told you to try (Recs) and the promises you made (Promises), and it lets you search, keep and export every message. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing about your messages leaves your Mac. Version 1.1 needs macOS 26 or later and costs {{lp:$20 once until 28 October 2026, then $39|$39 once}}, with every feature free for 14 days.     Keep

    Messages lets old photos go, but Textlore keeps its own copy.

    When your Mac runs low on space, Messages removes its copies of photos and files and leaves them only in iCloud. Textlore saves every message and photo into its own folder the moment it arrives, so a photo from today is still here in 10 years.

    The archive starts the day you install Textlore. One-time codes are skipped if you've told Messages to delete them.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Still wondering? Ask in The Useful Tech Club or read Help.

    Does it work with WhatsApp?

    Yes, with WhatsApp for Mac. Textlore imports your WhatsApp for Mac chats alongside iMessage, each with a badge, and the Sources filter shows one app or both. Recs, Promises, search, Ask AI and export cover WhatsApp too.

    Do Recs and Promises send my messages anywhere?

    No. Apple's on-device model finds them on your Mac, and nothing leaves it. Both are read only: Textlore never sends a message. They need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

    How long does the first scan take?

    On a long history, the first full scan can take hours. It runs in the background while you use Textlore, and this year's messages are ready first.

    I bought 1.0. Do I pay for 1.1?

    No. Every 1.x update is included with your licence. Choose Textlore > Check for Updates… to get it.

    Do I need my iPhone?

    No. Textlore reads the Messages history on your Mac. Turn on Messages in iCloud on your iPhone and Mac to have your full history there.

    Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

    macOS keeps your Messages history in a protected folder and only opens it to apps you switch on. Textlore only reads it.

    Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

    Apple doesn't let Mac App Store apps read the Messages history, so every app like this is sold directly.

    Can it show photos that are only in iCloud?

    It shows the preview Messages keeps on your Mac, and a button that opens the photo in Messages. From now on, the archive saves each photo while it's on your Mac.

    What happens after 14 days?

    Textlore asks for a licence. Your archive keeps saving in the background while you decide, so nothing is lost.

    Can I use it on my MacBook and my iMac?

    Yes. One licence works on 2 Macs.

    Can I get a refund?

    No. You can try every feature free for 14 days before you buy, so every sale is final. If something does not work, email raja@theusefultech.com and it gets fixed.

    What happens when version 2 comes out?

    Every 1.x update is included with your licence. Version 2.0 will be a discounted upgrade for people who own Textlore, never a subscription.
