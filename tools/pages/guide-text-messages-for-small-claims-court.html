{{icon:search}}1. Find the messages that prove it

  • Search for amounts like $1,200 or 1200, and words like deposit, paid, refund, invoice or finish.
  • Use Promises to list commitments with dates, like "I'll return the deposit by the 15th".
  • Use has:photo to find the photos of the damage or the work.
{{shot:promises|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|Textlore Promises listing commitments from chats with their dates.}}

{{icon:export}}2. Export them with context

  1. Select the key messages and the ones around them with Shift-click, then press ⌘E.
  2. Choose PDF, timestamps on, phone numbers on, photos full size.
  3. Export the whole conversation as a second file, in case the court or the other side asks for it.

{{icon:doc}}3. What to bring

  • The printed PDF, with a copy for the other side if your court asks for one.
  • Your phone, with the original conversation still on it.
  • Any receipts, contracts or bank records that match the messages.

They can help a lot, but the judge looks at all the evidence. Courts and countries differ, so check your court's guidance.
