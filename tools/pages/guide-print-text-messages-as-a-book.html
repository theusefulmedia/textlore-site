{{icon:rings}}1. Choose what goes in

  • The whole conversation for a full record, or 1 year for a lighter book.
  • A few special days: select them with Shift-click and export only those.
  • Use search to find favourites, like love you, proud of you or a nickname.

{{icon:export}}2. Make the PDF

  1. Open the conversation in Textlore and pick the dates.
  2. Choose PDF, bubble style, light, date headings on, photos full size, phone numbers off.
  3. Check the live preview, then export.
{{shot:export-options|(max-width: 960px) calc(100vw - 32px), 760px|The Textlore export options with a bubble-style preview of the conversation.}}

{{icon:doc}}3. Print it

  • At home: print double-sided and put it in a ring binder or a simple folder.
  • As a book: most print-on-demand services take a PDF upload. Check their page size, and choose a matching size in the print dialog.
  • Keep the PDF too. A file outlasts paper.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Can I include photos and stickers?

Photos that are on your Mac go in. Textlore keeps its own copy of photos from the day you install it.

Will phone numbers show in the book?

Not unless you switch them on. They are hidden by default.

Can I make a book from a group chat?

Yes. Each person's name shows above their messages.

Can I make a book from WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Import the WhatsApp chat export into Textlore and export it the same way.

How long does it take?

A few minutes for the PDF. Printing depends on the service you choose.
