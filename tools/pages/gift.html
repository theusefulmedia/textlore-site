Questions

The details.

What do they need?

A Mac with macOS 26 or later, signed in to Messages. WhatsApp chats work too. Textlore only reads messages. It never sends, edits or deletes one, and nothing leaves their Mac.

When does the gift arrive?

Gumroad emails it as soon as you pay. To give it on a set day, buy it that morning, or print the card now and buy on the day.

Can I gift Textlore Forever?

Yes. Textlore Forever ($79) covers every future major version. Tick "Give as a gift?" at checkout in the same way.

Can they try it first?

Anyone can download Textlore and use every feature free for 14 days. Your gift key unlocks it for good.

Can I give it to several people?

Yes. Each gift is its own checkout with its own recipient, and each person gets their own licence key.
