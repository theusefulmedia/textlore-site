Textlore vs iMazing, for your iMessages.

iMazing is a full iPhone manager that also exports messages. Textlore is a Mac app built around your Messages and WhatsApp history. They overlap on exporting texts, and differ on almost everything else.

Updated 22 October 2026. Prices and features checked on each maker's website.

{{icon:bolt}}Short answer

Pick iMazing if your texts are only on an iPhone, you use Windows, you need WhatsApp chats from an iPhone backup, or you want to back up and manage the whole phone. Pick Textlore if your messages are already on your Mac through Messages in iCloud or WhatsApp for Mac, and you want to see what friends told you to try and what you promised, and search, keep and export it all without making a backup each time, for a one-time price.

Not listed on its site means we couldn't find the feature on iMazing's website, not that it's missing. iMazing's Messages page lists PDF, Excel, CSV and text exports, printing with page numbers and timestamps, and bulk export of attachments. Check imazing.com for its current features and prices.

{{icon:check}}Where iMazing is the better choice

  • It works from the iPhone. iMazing reads messages from a backup of your iPhone or iPad, so it doesn't matter whether Messages in iCloud is on, or whether you own a Mac at all.
  • It runs on Windows. Textlore is Mac only.
  • It does far more. iMazing backs up the whole phone, transfers photos, music and files, manages apps, and moves your messages to a new iPhone. Textlore does none of that.
  • WhatsApp from the iPhone. iMazing exports and transfers WhatsApp chats from an iPhone backup. Textlore reads WhatsApp for Mac instead, so it sees the chats WhatsApp for Mac has on your Mac.
  • Excel files and print layout. iMazing exports straight to Excel, and its print options include paper size, page numbers and a timestamp.

{{icon:spark}}Where Textlore is the better choice

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your Messages and WhatsApp for Mac history. Recs lists what friends told you to try, with their exact words, and Promises lists what you said you'd do for people, both found by Apple's on-device model on Macs with Apple Intelligence. It also searches every year as you type, keeps its own archive of messages and photos, exports to 6 formats with names and numbers hidden, and adds Your Lore, your texting years as tree rings, and Ask AI. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Recs and Promises. Every book, show, restaurant and app friends told you to try, with who said it and their words, and every promise you made, with how many days late it is. iMazing doesn't list anything like them.
  • No backup step. Textlore reads your Mac's Messages history directly and updates within seconds when a new text arrives. iMazing works from a backup, so you back up again to see new messages.
  • Search built for messages. Type and results narrow with every letter, with filters such as from:, in:, before:, during: and has:photo. ⌥Space searches from any app.
  • Private exports. Phone numbers and emails are hidden unless you switch them on, and names can show in full, as initials, or not at all, with a live preview of the file.
  • More formats. PDF, HTML, plain text, Markdown, CSV, or an image of a few messages.
  • An archive that keeps going. Textlore saves every message and photo while it's on your Mac, so they stay after Messages deletes them or clears space.
  • Paid once. {{lp:$20 until 28 October, then $39|$39}} for 2 Macs, with every 1.x update included. iMazing is a yearly subscription.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • Recs, Promises and Ask AI need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

{{icon:help}}Questions

Is iMazing a one-time purchase?

No. iMazing's licensing policy says it stopped selling device licences in June 2025 and now only offers subscriptions, renewed yearly. Textlore is a one-time purchase.

Does iMazing need an iPhone backup to export messages?

Yes. iMazing reads messages from a backup of your iPhone or iPad that it makes or reads on your computer. Textlore reads the Messages history on your Mac, so there's no backup to make.

Can Textlore read an iPhone backup?

No. Textlore reads the Messages history on your Mac. To get iPhone texts there, switch on Messages in iCloud on your iPhone and your Mac.

Which is better for printing texts for court?

Both export a PDF. iMazing works from the iPhone itself and has detailed print options. Textlore exports a date range with timestamps and phone numbers switched on, with a live preview. Our guide on printing text messages for court covers the details.

Do Textlore and iMazing both read WhatsApp?

Yes, from different places. Textlore reads your chats from WhatsApp for Mac and shows them next to your iMessages. iMazing reads WhatsApp from an iPhone backup and can transfer it to a new phone.

Can I try Textlore before I pay?

Yes. Every feature is free for 14 days, with no account, no email and no card. After that it's a one-time purchase for 2 Macs.
