How to search WhatsApp messages on a Mac.

Type in the search field above your chat list in WhatsApp for Mac to search every chat at once. It only finds messages that synced to your Mac, so there are 3 ways to search, and each one misses something different.

Updated 17 November 2026. Textlore needs macOS 26 or later.

Quick answer

Open WhatsApp for Mac, click the search field above your chat list and type a word, a name or a phrase. To search messages that never synced to your Mac, export the chat from your iPhone, or use Textlore to search your WhatsApp for Mac chats and your iMessages together.

1. Search in WhatsApp for Mac

  1. Open WhatsApp on your Mac.
  2. Click the search field above your chat list and type a word, a name or a phrase.
  3. Matching chats and messages appear below the field. Click a message to jump to it in its chat.

WhatsApp for Mac holds the recent history your phone sent when you linked it, plus new messages. If a message is older than that, search for it in WhatsApp on your iPhone, where the search bar on the Chats tab looks through every chat.

2. Search an exported chat with Spotlight

  1. On your iPhone, open the chat in WhatsApp and tap the contact or group name at the top.
  2. Tap Export Chat, choose Without Media, then AirDrop it to your Mac.
  3. Open the .zip in your Downloads folder. Press Command-Space and type a word from the chat: Spotlight finds the .txt file, and Command-F in TextEdit finds the line.

Where it falls short

  • 1 chat at a time. There is no way to export every chat at once.
  • Long chats are cut short. WhatsApp keeps about the latest 40,000 messages without media.
  • No filters. You search plain text, with no way to narrow by person or date.

3. Search WhatsApp and iMessage together with Textlore

Textlore is a Mac app that reads the chats in WhatsApp for Mac and shows them next to your iMessage history, so 1 search covers both. It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  1. Install it. Download Textlore, open it and switch on Full Disk Access when it asks.
  2. Add WhatsApp. Open Settings > Sources and set up WhatsApp for Mac. Each WhatsApp chat carries a badge.
  3. Search. Type in the search bar. Results appear as you type, by word, person, date or photo, with matches lit in amber. Press Return to open the conversation at that message.
  4. Narrow it. The Sources filter shows iMessage, WhatsApp or both. Press Option-Space anywhere on your Mac to search without opening the main window.

Recs, Promises and export cover WhatsApp chats too (Recs and Promises need a Mac with Apple Intelligence), so a restaurant a friend sent you or a promise you made turns up whichever app it was in. Questions

Can I search all my WhatsApp chats at once on a Mac?

Yes. In WhatsApp for Mac, type in the search field above your chat list and it shows matching messages from every chat. It only covers the messages that have synced to your Mac.

Why can't WhatsApp for Mac find an old message?

WhatsApp for Mac holds the recent history your phone sent when you linked it, plus new messages. An older message may only be on your iPhone, so search there too.

Can I search WhatsApp and iMessage together?

Not in either app. Textlore shows your WhatsApp for Mac chats next to your iMessage history, so 1 search covers both, and the Sources filter narrows it to one app.

Does Textlore change my WhatsApp messages?

No. It only reads. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing about your messages leaves your Mac.
