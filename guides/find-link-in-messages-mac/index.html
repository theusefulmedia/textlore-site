How to find a link someone sent you in Messages on a Mac, 4 ways.

Open the conversation, click the person's name at the top and click Links: every link from that chat is in 1 list. If you don't remember who sent it, Safari, Messages search and Textlore look across every chat.

Updated 3 November 2026. For Messages on macOS 26 and later.

On this page

Quick answer 1. Conversation Links 2. Shared with You 3. Messages search 4. Textlore Questions

Quick answer

In Messages on your Mac, open the chat with the person who sent the link, click their name or picture at the top, then click Links. Click a link to open it. If you don't know which chat it's in, open Safari and click Shared with You in the sidebar, or search Messages for a word from the page and press Return.

1. Open the Links in a conversation

Each conversation in Messages keeps its links in 1 list, next to its photos.

  1. Open Messages and click the conversation with the person who sent the link.
  2. Click the person's name or picture at the top of the conversation. For a group, click the group name or picture.
  3. Click Links. Every link shared in that chat shows with its preview.
  4. Click a link to open it in your browser.

Where it falls short

  • You need to know the sender. If you can't remember who sent it, you open conversation after conversation.
  • No dates to jump to. A link from 3 years ago means a long scroll down the list.
  • Only what's on this Mac. If Keep messages is set to 30 Days or 1 Year in Messages > Settings > General, older links were removed with their messages.

2. Check Shared with You in Safari

Safari collects the links people send you in Messages, such as videos, news stories and recipes, so you can open them without finding the chat.

  1. Open Safari. If the sidebar is hidden, click the Sidebar button in the toolbar.
  2. Click Shared with You in the sidebar.
  3. Click a link to open it. Click the sender's name under it to reply in Messages.

If a link isn't there

  • Only people in your Contacts. Links from a number that isn't saved in Contacts don't appear.
  • Safari must be switched on. Open Messages > Settings > Shared with You and check that Safari is selected.
  • Only Messages links. A link sent in WhatsApp or by email won't show here.

3. Search Messages for the link

Messages search looks through every conversation on your Mac and puts links in their own group of results.

  1. Click the search field at the top of the Messages sidebar.
  2. Type a word you remember: the site's name, such as youtube or airbnb, or a word from the page's title.
  3. Press Return. Results are grouped into conversations, messages, links, photos, locations and documents.
  4. Click a link result to open it.

There's no way to ask Messages for links from 1 person or 1 year, so a common word like recipe can bring back a long list. Our guide on searching old iMessages has more on what Messages search can and can't find.

Questions

Why is a link missing from Shared with You?

Shared with You only shows links from people in your Contacts, and only when Safari is selected in Messages > Settings > Shared with You. Save the sender as a contact, or open the chat's Links instead.

Can I see every link one person sent me?

Yes, for 1 chat. Click their name at the top of the conversation and click Links. To include group chats they're in too, search from:Name has:link in Textlore.

Can I find links from my iPhone texts on my Mac?

Yes, once Messages in iCloud is on for your iPhone and your Mac. Your Mac then has the same conversations, and every way on this page finds their links.

Can I find a link someone sent me on WhatsApp?

Not in Messages or Safari. Textlore imports your chats from WhatsApp for Mac, so a has:link search finds WhatsApp links next to iMessage ones.
