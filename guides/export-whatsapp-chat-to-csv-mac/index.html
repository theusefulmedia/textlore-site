Short answer

WhatsApp only exports chats as a text file. On a Mac you can turn that file into a CSV with 1 Terminal command for free, or export the chat straight to CSV in Textlore, with dates, names and messages in their own columns.

TL;DR

  • On your iPhone, open the chat, tap its name, then Export Chat and Without Media. You get a zip with a .txt file.
  • 1 sed command turns each line into date, time, name and message columns that Numbers and Excel open.
  • Textlore reads WhatsApp for Mac and exports any chat or date range to CSV, next to your iMessages.
  • Phone numbers stay hidden in Textlore exports unless you switch them on.

1. Convert WhatsApp's export, free

  1. On your iPhone, open the chat, tap the name at the top, scroll down and tap Export Chat, then Without Media. AirDrop the zip to your Mac and open it.
  2. Open Terminal in the folder with _chat.txt and run:
sed -E -e 's/"/""/g' -e 's/^\[([^,]+), ([^]]+)\] ([^:]+): (.*)$/"\1","\2","\3","\4"/' _chat.txt > chat.csv

Open chat.csv in Numbers or Excel: date, time, name and message each get a column. It is free. A message with line breaks spills its extra lines into new rows, and the date order follows your phone's region settings.

Questions

Can WhatsApp export to CSV?

No. WhatsApp exports a chat as a .txt file, with or without media. You can convert that file to CSV, or use an app such as Textlore that exports CSV directly.

How do I open a WhatsApp chat in Excel?

Convert _chat.txt to CSV with the sed command on this page, then open chat.csv in Excel or Numbers.

Why are some messages split over several rows?

WhatsApp's text export puts each line of a multi-line message on its own line. Textlore's CSV keeps each message in 1 row.

Does Textlore need my phone?

No. It reads WhatsApp for Mac on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac.
