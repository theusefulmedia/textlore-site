1. Print to PDF from Messages

  1. Open the group chat and scroll up until the oldest part you need has loaded.
  2. Choose File > Print, then click the PDF menu and Save as PDF.

It is free and needs nothing extra. Messages only prints what it has loaded, so long group chats come out cut off, and every phone number in the group prints as it is.

2. imessage-exporter, free

imessage-exporter is a free, open-source command-line tool. Install it with Homebrew, give Terminal Full Disk Access, then run:

brew install imessage-exporter
imessage-exporter -f html -o ~/Desktop/messages

You get a folder with a file for every conversation, group chats included, with attachments copied next to them. It exports everything at once, so you dig the group you want out of the folder, and there is no preview.

3. Export 1 group in Textlore

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your Messages and WhatsApp for Mac history. Open the group, or search in:Book Club to find it, then press ⇧⌘E.

  • 6 formats. PDF, HTML, plain text, Markdown, CSV, or an image of a few messages.

  • Just a stretch. Export a date range, or click Select and pick the messages you need.
  • Live preview. See exactly what the file will look like before you save it.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • It only reads. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.
