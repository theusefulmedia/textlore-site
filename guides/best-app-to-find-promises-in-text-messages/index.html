1. Search the Messages app

  1. Open Messages and click the search field at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Type a phrase, such as I promise or I'll pay, and press Return.
  3. Click a result. The conversation opens at that message, with the messages around it.

Messages has no date filter, and common phrases bring back long lists. Check that Keep messages is set to Forever and that Messages in iCloud has finished syncing, or old texts will be missing. See how to search old iMessages on a Mac.

2. Search the database in Terminal

Messages keeps your history in a database file that macOS's sqlite3 tool can search. It is free, but you type commands, it shows numbers instead of names, and on recent macOS many messages store their text in a coded column, so some results come back blank. The steps are in our guide to searching old iMessages.

3. Let Textlore list them

Textlore is a Mac app that reads your Messages and WhatsApp for Mac history. Recs lists what friends told you to try, with their exact words, and Promises lists what you said you'd do for people, both found by Apple's on-device model on Macs with Apple Intelligence. It also searches every year as you type, keeps its own archive of messages and photos, and exports to 6 formats with phone numbers and emails hidden by default. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Promises you made

Open Promises with ⌘6. It lists every time you said you would do something for someone, with the day you said it and how many days late it is. Promises past their day come first. Click one to read the message. Use I kept this, Remind me or Open in Messages. When a later message suggests you kept it, Textlore tells you so you can check it off.

Promises someone else made

Promises shows what you promised. To find another person's, press ⌘F and search:

  • from:Jordan "I promise" for Jordan's exact words.
  • from:Jordan "I'll pay" after:2025 for a date range.
  • from:Jordan "by friday" -cancel to leave out messages that say cancel.

Export the chat with dates as a PDF with ⇧⌘E. See how to print text messages for court.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • Recs, Promises and Ask AI need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

How do I find a promise someone made in my texts?

Search for the person and the phrase. In Textlore, type from:Name "I promise" in search. In Messages, search the phrase and scroll to the person's messages.

Does Textlore find promises other people made?

No. Promises lists what you said you would do. To find another person's promise, use search with from:Name and a phrase.

Can I see how late a promise is?

Yes. Promises in Textlore shows the day you said it and how many days late it is, and tells you when a later message suggests you kept it.

Can I use texts as proof of a promise?

Courts often accept printed texts, but the rules differ and the other side can challenge them. Keep the original conversation and ask a lawyer.

Yes. Textlore imports WhatsApp for Mac chats, and Promises and search cover them.
