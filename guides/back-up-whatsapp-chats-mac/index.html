How to back up WhatsApp chats on a Mac.

WhatsApp backs up to iCloud from your iPhone, but you can't open that backup on a Mac. These are 4 ways to keep a copy of your chats with your Mac, and what each one misses.

Updated 13 November 2026. For WhatsApp on iPhone and WhatsApp for Mac on macOS 26 and later.

On this page

Quick answer 1. iCloud Chat Backup 2. Export Chat 3. Finder backup 4. Textlore Questions

Quick answer

Turn on Settings > Chats > Chat Backup in WhatsApp on your iPhone so you can always restore to a new phone. To keep chats you can read on your Mac, use Export Chat for the chats that matter, or let Textlore show your WhatsApp for Mac chats next to your iMessages and export them to files.

1. Back up to iCloud from your iPhone

  1. On your iPhone, open WhatsApp and go to Settings > Chats > Chat Backup.
  2. Tap Back Up Now. Turn on Auto Backup to repeat it daily, weekly or monthly.

This is the backup WhatsApp restores when you set up a new phone. It needs enough free iCloud storage, and it only opens inside WhatsApp on a phone, so it gives you nothing to read on your Mac.

2. Export a chat to your Mac

  1. On your iPhone, open the chat in WhatsApp and tap the contact or group name at the top.
  2. Scroll down, tap Export Chat and choose Attach Media or Without Media.
  3. Choose AirDrop and pick your Mac. The .zip lands in your Downloads folder with a .txt file of the chat inside, plus photos if you attached media.

Where it falls short

  • 1 chat at a time. There is no way to export every chat at once.
  • Long chats are cut short. WhatsApp keeps about the latest 10,000 messages with media, or about 40,000 without.
  • Plain text. The .txt file is hard to search across many chats.

3. Back up your whole iPhone in Finder

  1. Connect your iPhone to your Mac with a cable and select it in the Finder sidebar.
  2. In the General tab, choose Back up all of the data on your iPhone to this Mac, then click Back Up Now.

The backup includes WhatsApp along with everything else on your phone. It's a good safety net, but you can't open or restore WhatsApp on its own from it without another app such as iMazing.

Questions

Can I open my WhatsApp iCloud backup on a Mac?

No. The iCloud backup can only be restored into WhatsApp on a phone. To read chats on your Mac, export them or use WhatsApp for Mac.

Does exporting a chat include every message?

Not always. WhatsApp caps exports at about 10,000 of the latest messages with media and about 40,000 without, so very long chats are cut short.

Does a Finder backup of my iPhone include WhatsApp?

Yes, a full backup to your Mac includes WhatsApp data. You can't open or restore WhatsApp on its own from it without another app.
