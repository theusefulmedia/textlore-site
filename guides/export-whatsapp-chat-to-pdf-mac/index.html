How to export a WhatsApp chat to PDF on a Mac, 3 ways.

Turn a WhatsApp conversation into a PDF you can keep, print or send. 2 ways are free, and 1 exports whole chats from WhatsApp for Mac with no phone and no backup.

Updated 17 November 2026. For WhatsApp for Mac and macOS 26.

Quick answer

In WhatsApp on your phone, open the chat, tap the contact or group name and choose Export Chat. Send the file to your Mac, open the text file in TextEdit, choose File > Print and pick Save as PDF. That works for short chats. For a long chat, a date range, chat bubbles or a PDF with numbers hidden, export it from WhatsApp for Mac with an app such as Textlore.

1. Use Export Chat, then print the file to PDF

WhatsApp's own export runs on your phone and gives you a text file. Your Mac turns that file into a PDF.

  1. Open WhatsApp on your iPhone and open the chat.
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top, scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without Media for a smaller file, or Attach Media to include photos as separate files.
  4. AirDrop the file to your Mac, or save it to Files and open it there. Unzip it if it arrives as a .zip.
  5. Open the text file in TextEdit, choose File > Print or press ⌘P, open the PDF menu and choose Save as PDF.

Where it falls short

  • It stops at a cap. WhatsApp sends the latest 10,000 messages with media, or up to 40,000 without. A chat older than that loses its start.
  • No bubbles. Each message is a line of text with a date, a time and a name. Photos sit in separate files, not next to the messages they came with.
  • No date range. You get the whole chat and delete the pages you don't need in Preview.
  • Nothing is hidden. Phone numbers of people not in your contacts print as they are.

The 10,000 and 40,000 message limits come from WhatsApp's Help Center.

2. Screenshot WhatsApp for Mac and join the images into a PDF

For 5 or 10 messages, screenshots are quick and keep the bubbles exactly as they look.

  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp for Mac and scroll to the messages you want.
  2. Press ⇧⌘4 and drag over them. Each screenshot is saved to your desktop.
  3. Scroll and repeat until you have every part you need.
  4. Select the screenshots in Finder, Control-click and choose Quick Actions > Create PDF. Open the PDF and check the pages are in order.

Where it falls short

  • It doesn't scale. A long chat turns into dozens of files, and every one needs cropping.
  • The text is a picture. You can't reliably search or copy it, so a PDF kept as a record is hard to check.
  • Private details stay in. You blur numbers by hand in every image.

Can I export a WhatsApp chat to PDF for free?

Yes. Use Export Chat in WhatsApp on your phone to get a text file, open it on your Mac in TextEdit, then choose File > Print and Save as PDF. You get plain text with a line per message, not chat bubbles.

Is there a limit on how many WhatsApp messages I can export?

WhatsApp's own Export Chat caps it. With media it sends the latest 10,000 messages, and without media up to 40,000. Older messages in a long chat are left out.

Does Textlore need my iPhone or a backup?
