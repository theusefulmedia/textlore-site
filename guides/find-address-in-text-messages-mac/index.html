In Messages on your Mac, click the search field at the top of the sidebar, type the street name or building and press Return. Click a result to open the conversation at that message. If it doesn't show up, check that Keep messages is set to Forever and that Messages in iCloud is on.

1. Search Messages for a word from the address

The search field in Messages looks through every conversation on your Mac, not just the one that's open. Addresses are long and easy to misspell, so search for the 1 word you're sure of.

  1. Open Messages and click the search field at the top of the sidebar.
  2. Type the street name, the building or the area. Messages suggests matching conversations, photos, links and places as you type.
  3. Add another word if the list is long.
  4. Press Return to see all results, grouped into conversations, links, photos, locations and documents.
  5. Click a result. The conversation opens at that message, with the messages before and after it.

Messages can also find text inside photos, so a screenshot of an address or a photo of a sign can turn up too.

Where it falls short

  • No date filter. You can't ask for the address Jordan sent in 2022. See how to search iMessages by date if you need that.
  • Common words flood it. Searching for road or street in years of texts brings back hundreds of messages.
  • It only searches this Mac. If Keep messages is set to 30 Days or 1 Year, older conversations were removed.

2. Search with Spotlight

Spotlight is free and already on your Mac. Apple's Spotlight page lists messages among the things it finds, along with apps, files and emails.

  1. Press ⌘Space to open Spotlight.
  2. Type the street or building name.
  3. Click a Messages result to open it.

If no Messages results show, open System Settings and check Spotlight's search results. Spotlight describes no sender or date filters, so it works best when the street name is unusual. See Textlore vs Spotlight for the full comparison.

3. Find it by person and date in Textlore

Textlore is a Mac app that searches your whole Messages history and your chats from WhatsApp for Mac as you type. You can search by who sent it and when, which is how most people remember an address: “Jordan sent it when they moved, around 2022.” It only reads: it never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  1. Install it. Download Textlore, open it and switch on Full Disk Access when it asks. macOS keeps your Messages history in a protected folder, and this lets Textlore read it. About 100,000 messages import in under a minute.
  2. Press ⌘F and type the sender and a word you remember. from:Jordan flat finds every message from Jordan that mentions a flat. Your words are lit up in amber.
  1. Narrow it to a time. Add during:summer 2024, before:2023 or after:march 2021. Use in:Family for 1 group chat, or has:photo if they sent a screenshot of the address.
  2. Open it in context. Use ↑ and ↓ to move through results and press Return to open the conversation at that message.
  3. Search from any app. Press ⌥Space anywhere and a small search bar floats over your work, so you can find the address while you're typing it into Maps.

If you can't remember a word at all, Ask AI on Macs with Apple Intelligence answers questions such as “What's Jordan's new address?” with Apple's on-device model, and lists the messages it used.

Questions

Can I find an address someone texted me years ago?

Yes, if the message is still on your Mac. Check Messages > Settings > General > Keep messages is set to Forever, and that Messages in iCloud is on so your iPhone texts are on your Mac too.

They sent a screenshot of the address. Can I still find it?

Often, yes. Messages can find text inside photos, so searching for the street name can turn up the screenshot. In Textlore, add has:photo to see only messages with a photo.

I don't remember the street name. What do I search?

Search for the person and a word people use with addresses, such as flat, apartment, address, postcode or the name of the area. In Textlore, from:Name address with a during: date narrows it fast.

Can I find an address someone sent me on WhatsApp?

Not in Messages or Spotlight. Textlore imports your chats from WhatsApp for Mac, so the same search finds WhatsApp messages next to iMessage ones.
