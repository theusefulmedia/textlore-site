Short answer

To find what 1 person texted you on a Mac, pick them in Messages search and add a word, or use from: in Textlore, which also adds dates, group chats, photos and links in 1 search.

TL;DR

  • In Messages, type the name, pick the person from the suggestions, then add a word.
  • Terminal can list every text from 1 number for free, with a catch on newer messages.

1. Pick them in Messages search

  1. Click the search field at the top of the Messages sidebar.
  2. Type the person's name and click them under the suggestions, so they become a filter.
  3. Type a word after it and press Return. Click a result to jump to it in the chat.

It is free and quick for 1 word. It can't add a date, limit to 1 group chat, or show only messages they sent rather than ones you sent them.

2. Query it in Terminal

Switch on Full Disk Access for Terminal in System Settings > Privacy & Security, then run this with their number:

sqlite3 ~/Library/Messages/chat.db "SELECT datetime(m.date/1000000000 + 978307200, 'unixepoch', 'localtime'), m.text FROM message m JOIN handle h ON m.handle_id = h.ROWID WHERE h.id LIKE '%5551234%' AND m.is_from_me = 0 AND m.text LIKE '%cabin%' ORDER BY m.date;"

It is free and lists every match with a date. Newer macOS versions often store the words in a different column, so many recent messages come back blank or go missing.

3. from: in Textlore

Press ⌘F and type from: with a name. Each search word turns into a token, and results narrow with every letter:

  • from:Jordan cabin finds Jordan's messages about the cabin.
  • from:Mom during:christmas 2025 finds what Mom sent last Christmas.
  • from:Theo in:Book Club finds Theo's messages in 1 group chat.
  • from:Priya has:link or has:photo finds the links or photos she sent.

Return opens the conversation at that message. Press ⌥Space in any app for quick search without opening Textlore first.

What Textlore doesn't do

  • It is Mac only, and needs macOS 26 or later.
  • It reads the Messages history on your Mac, so your iPhone texts need Messages in iCloud switched on to get there.
  • It reads Messages (iMessage, SMS and the other texts in it) and WhatsApp for Mac. It can't read an iPhone backup.
  • It only reads. It never sends, edits or deletes a message, and nothing leaves your Mac.
