Your messages stay on your Mac

Textlore reads your Messages history on your Mac and keeps its own copy in ~/Library/Application Support/Textlore. That data never leaves your Mac. Textlore never sends, edits or deletes a message.

No account, no tracking

Textlore has no account, no analytics and no tracking. This website has none either: no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

The 2 services it contacts

  • Gumroad, to check your licence key. It sends the key and the product ID.
  • textlore.app, to check for updates. It sends the app version.

Nothing else is sent.

Ask AI

Ask AI uses Apple's on-device model. Your questions and messages are not sent anywhere.

Payment

Gumroad handles payment and keeps the details you give at checkout, under Gumroad's own privacy policy. Gumroad's checkout loads on this website only when you reach for a Buy button.

Removing everything

To remove everything, use Settings > Privacy > Delete All App Data, then move Textlore to the Bin.
